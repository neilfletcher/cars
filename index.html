Since 2003 I've been posting on PistonHeads, and since 2008 on the beex forums. Between them they hold a fairly complete, fairly embarrassing record of every car I've bought, broken, fixed, regretted and sold. This page pulls it all together.

The pattern is easy to spot. A sensible car gets bought, then something loud or fast or enormous replaces it, then a bill arrives, then a cheap barge turns up on eBay. Repeat for thirty-odd years.

Some overlapped, some were household runarounds rather than mine alone, and for the early ones the dates are best guesses. Where I've quoted myself, those are my own words from the forums at the time, typos tidied.

The rules I've learned the hard way: a £1,000 Lexus LS400 is the best car you can buy, a £15,500 Cerbera is not a £15,500 car, and there is always room for another MX-5.

Verdicts

With hindsight, and receipts.

Favourite ever
Honda NSX

Reliable, sublime, the best gearbox ever made. Sold at nearly 100,000 miles and still missed.

Most expensive to run
TVR Cerbera 4.2

£10,875 in year one, including £3,200 of petrol. About £7k more later for an engine and gearbox.

Best value
Lexus LS400

Bought twice, for £1,000 and then £595. Nothing at any price feels as calm at 100mph.

Best deal
Jaguar XJR

Bought for £1,000, an ex-Evo fast fleet car, sold seven months later for £1,300 with a broken diff.

Most complete
Lotus Evora S

The best everyday driver's car I've had. Shame about the boot filling with water.

The one I miss
Toyota MR2 Turbo

Of all the early cars, still the one I'd have back first.
